Dr. Fawzi Abdulaziz Albalooshi

Associate Professor

Computer Science
+973 17437720
S40-1075
falblooshi@uob.edu.bh

Areas of Expertise

Software Engineering

Education

PhD Software Engineering

Biography

Dr Fawzi Albalooshi is currently a faculty member in the computer science department at the College of IT, University of Bahrain. Member of the SEAR Board since 2019. He worked on a secondment basis for three years as a Senior Director in the Higher Education Review Unit in the National Authority for Qualifications and Quality Assurance of Education and Training. He has years of academic and administrative experience in the field of IT. He has been the Acting Dean for the College of Applied Studies, Director of Business and IT programs for the College of Applied Studies, Director Evening Program for the IT College, and Director Evening Program at the College of Science. He was a member (and chaired some) of many committees at the national, university, college, and department levels; and published many research articles in international journals, and conferences and authored and edited books in the field of IT. He earned his PhD from the University of Wales in the field of Software Engineering.
